LUCKER PARTY - PROTOTYPE 01.1

Extract the entire ZIP before running LuckerParty.exe (Windows) or
LuckerParty.x86_64 (Linux). Keep the executable, PCK, and data folder together.
No Godot editor or .NET SDK is required for the packaged build.

CONTROLS
WASD or arrow keys: move
Mouse: look
Left Shift: sprint
Space: jump
R: reset to spawn
Escape: release mouse and open menu; Escape again or Resume returns to play
Alt-Tab: opens menu and releases the mouse
Quit: use the menu or close the window
F3: show frame timing and physics/camera diagnostics
F4: toggle camera position interpolation (ON by default)
F5: toggle physics between 60 Hz (normal) and 10 Hz (diagnostic only)

WHAT TO CHECK
You should see red, blue, yellow, and green boxes on a gray grid floor.
Walk around and into boxes: they should block your movement.
Try jumping onto the low green step.
Diagonal movement should have the same speed as moving straight.
Try sprinting, looking up/down, resetting, and switching away from the window.
Resize the window and check that the interface stays readable.

JITTER COMPARISON
Hold A or D with the mouse still and watch a box edge. Press F4 to compare the
original stepped camera position with smooth interpolation. F5 at 10 Hz makes
the difference easier to see; return to 60 Hz for normal play.
At 240 FPS, normal frame time is about 4.17 ms. F3 shows average, p95, and maximum
frame times over the most recent 240 frames. Startup or Alt-Tab can temporarily
inflate these values; wait for the sample window to settle before comparing.
If artifacts persist with interpolation ON, report whether frame times spike
and whether the edges jump in position or leave a trailing/ghost image.

This is a local single-player test bed. Multiplayer and minigames come later.
When reporting a problem, include your Windows/GPU versions, what you did,
what happened, and whether resetting or restarting fixed it.
